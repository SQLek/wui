Check the extruder has filament in it and the hotend is heated to an correct temperature.

Use a sharpie, pen etc. and mark the filament at 120mm from the closest to extruder point available.
This is for easier measurment.

Extrude 100mm of filament you should use this command: 
G91;
G1 E100 F60;

Note 100mm as Extrude distance. Wait for your printer to finish extruding (it should take less than 2m).
We are doing this slowly to get most precise results.


Use the digital calipers to measure distance to the mark and substract it from 120.
This is your actual extrude length ie. 120-22=98 or 120-18=102 so 98 or 102 is your value to enter.
